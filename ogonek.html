Ogonek

A free browser game by Mad Gever (Artem). No download, no sign-up.

A burner festival on a Bali mountain, and you have a flamethrower. Burning dancers spread the fire: burn every camp down, beat the DJ, then leave no trace.

Play Ogonek free

Controls

  • WASD to move, aim with the mouse, hold the mouse button to fire.
  • Hold E at the generator to refuel. R restarts, Esc goes back to the site.
  • On a phone: stick on the left, fire on the right (it aims for you), hold the button at the generator to refuel.
